# AI notes

Cursor's coding agent wrote most of the chunker, the Chroma indexing, the hybrid fusion, and the answer formatting. I used it to implement a decision after the eval numbers existed, not to pick the number. It was wrong about retrieval quality: a higher fusion score was treated as evidence that the gold page had been found. On `fb_00735` the top scores are signature and certification pages in the right company, while the legal-proceedings page (PepsiCo 2022 10-K, page 26) stays outside the top 5. That only showed up by scoring page hit against the labeled pages in `eval/results.json`, not by reading the score. The same check is why hybrid search stayed even though it did not beat BM25 on page hit@5 (both 16/30): document hit and page MRR moved, page hit did not, and the write-up says so.
